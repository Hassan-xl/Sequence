        How to play

        Every card except the Jacks appears twice on the board. The four corners are free squares that count for everyone.

        Your turn

        • Pick a card from your hand. Its two squares light up.
        • Tap a lit square, then tap it again (or press Place chip) to claim it. You draw a new card automatically.
        • If both squares for a card are taken, it is dead. Swap it for a fresh card, once per turn.

        Jacks

        Chips in a finished sequence are locked and cannot be removed.

        Winning

        • A sequence is five of your chips in a row: across, down or diagonal.
        • First to 2 sequences: two teams need two sequences, three teams need one. First to get there wins.
        • Full board: keep playing until the board is full or the cards run out. The team with the most sequences wins. If that's tied, the team with more chips on the board wins.
        • Your two sequences may share one chip.
        • End game stops the game at any time (online, only the host can). The team with the most sequences wins, then the most chips on the board.
